PLUMERIA CLUSTER - SINGLE-PIECE PRINTABLE RELIEF

Based on the supplied reference: three overlapping five-petal plumeria flowers,
with six curved, pointed and veined leaves. No pot, separate stems, assembly,
pegs, or featureless backing disk. The backside is deliberately flat and the
petal undersides are filled in, so this is a sculptural decorative relief.

PRINT FILE
Plumeria_Single_Piece_165mm.stl
Units: millimeters. Size: 165.0 x 159.83 x 17.35 mm.
Place the broad flat back on the build plate, detailed face upward. The file
already has the flat back at Z=0. Fits a 180 x 180 mm bed at 100% scale.
The mesh is one connected, closed solid. No assembly or gluing required.

STARTING SETTINGS FOR A 0.4 MM NOZZLE
- PLA or matte PLA; choose filament according to the intended environment.
- 0.16 mm layers for the veins and curved flower faces; 0.12 mm for finer detail.
- 3 walls, 5 top and 4 bottom layers, around 15% gyroid infill.
- Supports OFF as a starting point. Every shape is supported by its own solid
  underside; there are no hanging petal sheets, bridges, or floating bodies.
- A 3 mm brim is optional if leaf tips lift. At full size, this still fits within
  a 180 mm square bed, but confirm the usable bed area in your printer profile.
- Use the normal temperature and cooling profile for your actual filament.
- Do not use vase/spiral mode. Print the entire object together.
- Do not scale down aggressively: leaf outline thickness is designed around
  2 mm and the clipped tip width around 1.7 mm at the original design size.

Before printing, open the STL in Bambu Studio or your slicer. Confirm dimensions,
orientation, bed clearance and continuous layers. Inspect the fine veins and
flower-center toolpaths. No physical test print or printer-specific slicing has
been performed; geometry validation is not a guarantee of print success.
The overall solid volume is 94.006 cm3; actual filament use depends on slicing.

PAINTING
Use a light primer in thin coats so the leaf veins remain visible. Suggested
reference-inspired colors: green leaves, ivory petals, golden-yellow centers,
and pale blush-pink at selected petal rims. Let the print cool before removal;
handle the leaf tips gently. Finish one side at a time if applying a clear coat.

EDITABLE SOURCE
Plumeria_Single_Piece_Editable.blend contains the actual optimized final mesh,
plus a hidden collection of individually named source petals, flower centers
and veined leaves. Studio floor, camera and lights are not printable.
Scene units are millimeters using metric scale_length 0.001.
The build_relief.py procedural source recreates the original high-resolution
union and source pieces. render_relief.py then optimizes, saves and renders it.
Run build_relief.py BEFORE render_relief.py; otherwise rerunning the rendering
script alone would simplify the already-optimized STL again.
Python dependencies: numpy, scipy, trimesh, manifold3d, networkx.
Rendering/editing: Blender 4.3 or newer.
